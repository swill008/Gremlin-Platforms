# Help review H6: Device Mapping Editor chapter

File: `qml/help/device_mapping_editor.js` (651 lines, 33 topics). Rendered by
`qml/DialogHelp.qml` as Qt rich text (`TextEdit.RichText`): `<p>`, `<h4>`,
`<ul>`/`<ol>`, `<b>`, links. Read-only review, 2026-11-22. Nothing was edited.

## Top 5 problems

1. **Walls of text in key topics.** The Hotspot settings (line 261), the leader
   paragraph (268), Device Map Options' opening paragraph (100), the menu list
   (81-88), Print & Export's Scale (593) and Reset Picture (495) each pack
   10 or more facts into one paragraph or bullet. These are the hardest parts of
   the chapter to scan.
2. **"Photo" is still used for the background.** The glossary bans "Photo" for
   the plane (line 117: use **Background**) and for the device's picture
   (line 17: **Device Image**). Help says "onto the photo", "on the photo" and
   "the device's photo" about 20 times (lines 15, 17, 47, 166, 221, 260, 285,
   363-364, 376, 460, 470, 496 and others).
3. **Inconsistent command names and capitals.** Glossary D15 says Title Case
   for menu and button names. Help uses "Delete leader" (268) next to
   "Delete Leader" (86), "Fill color…" (397, 576) next to "Fill Color…" (229),
   "Chip style" (337) next to "Chip Style" (228), "Turn group" (285) and
   "Paint format" (453). The Insert menu is listed as **Picture…** (84) but is
   called **Import Picture…** elsewhere (488; program map 07 line 165).
4. **Likely wrong instruction for Chip only (line 235).** It says to add a
   hotspot later with "Hotspot › Hide Hotspot". Hide cannot add something. It
   also says Chip only is "also in Device Map Options", but the Options topic
   (lines 102-108) doesn't list it.
5. **Much repetition across topics.** Cut is explained 4 times (92, 196, 236,
   344). The panel keys F4/F6/F7 are given 3 times (66, 73, 142). "Undo is on
   every right-click menu" appears twice (54, 195). "An unplugged stick still
   exports" appears twice (120, 607). Each repeat makes the pages longer without
   telling the reader anything new.

Counts: high 12, medium 47, low 30 (plus 2 notes that mark text as good, at 240 and 614).
