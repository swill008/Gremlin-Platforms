# Help review H7: Device Library and Logical Device chapters (2026-11-22)

Reviewer: agent H7 (read-only). Files: `qml/help/device_library.js`,
`qml/help/logical_device.js`. Checked against `claude/help-style.md`,
`claude/glossary.md` and `claude/program-map/10-device-library.md` (spot checks
only). Help renders as Qt RichText (`qml/DialogHelp.qml:849`), so `<h4>`,
`<ul>`, `<ol>`, `<b>`, `<i>` and links all work; nothing here needs new markup.

Severity: **high** = confusing or wrong; **medium** = hard to read; **low** = polish.

## Top 5 problems

1. **Walls of text in reference topics** (Device Library window, menus, saved
   setups, delete; several Common questions). One `<li>` or `<p>` carries
   4-6 ideas and runs 50-100 words. Split into sub-headed lists.
2. **Sentences far over 25 words**, often stacked with colons and semicolons
   (about 30 found; the worst are 427, 431, 116, 80, 300 in device_library.js).
3. **Internal or unexplained words**: "wires", "version 14 profile", "a row
   of the library's own list that can't be read ... system log", "claimed",
   "No hardware writer", "Show written by", "caret", and the data-flow arrow
   written with the menu-path mark "›".
4. **"pack" against the glossary**: glossary line 120 says the import/export
   wording is "file", never "pack", but the chapter mixes "device file" and
   "pack" (100, 107, 391-393, 427, 456; logical_device.js 50).
5. **Repetition and possible staleness**: the divider drag, "menus leave out
   what doesn't apply", and "Clear Setup is Delete Device" are each said 3-5
   times; Tidy says "No second question follows" while the map
   (10-device-library.md:53) says Tidy's Remove asks a shared question
   (unverified; check the window).

Counts: high 12, medium 42, low 20 (74 findings).
