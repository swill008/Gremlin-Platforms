## What's new in 1.0.34

### New
- **Search** (magnifying glass on the toolbar, after Input Tester; also Tools › Mapping › Search…, Ctrl+Shift+F): find every use of an input, an output (vJoy or Xbox control), a mode or an action type across the whole profile, including macro steps, conditions and mode switches. Results are grouped by kind of use (e.g. Sends to this output, Checks this output, Switches to this mode) with counts, and broken ones show [binding output not found]. Click a result to open that device, mode and row; ‹ Previous / Next › step through them. **Find uses…** on a row's right-click menu (and on vJoy Viewer chips, Xbox Output View rows and Manage Modes) opens Search filled in
- **Bindings on every row**: on Device Bindings, Keyboard, Logical Device and OSC, each row that has bindings shows the word **Bindings**, the count **[n]** and one box per target (e.g. Bindings [2] [vJoy 1 · Button 1] [Xbox 360 Controller · A]), lined up in one column. Too many end in "…" with every target on hover; a row with no bindings shows nothing
  - **Appearance › Bindings** sets how they look: the word, the number and the boxes (size, font, colors, border, square or rounded corners, spacing), with a reset to defaults
- **Group details and Row details** in Appearance: choose what the group lines and rows tell you
  - **Group details**: **Controls by type**, **Bindings by type** (e.g. "29 buttons [30 bindings] · 6 axes [6 bindings] · 1 hat [0 bindings]"), **No bindings**, **Binding output not found**, **Output devices**, **Action types**, **Custom names**, **Other modes**, **Wrap details** and **Hide empty**
  - **Row details**: **Bindings and targets**, **Action types**, **Binding output not found**, **Other modes**, **System name**, **Has a condition**, **No bindings** and **Wrap details**
  - Help has charts for both, and for the words they use
- **Each device keeps its own Appearance on Device Bindings**: switching device with the arrows loads its look; a device with none uses the **shared Device Bindings look**
  - **Apply to all devices** makes the shown look the shared one for every device
  - **Copy from device…** loads the shared look or another device's look onto the shown device; **Save Appearance** keeps it
- **Xbox Setup** (on the Xbox card's menu and in the command palette): the ViGEmBus check and the **Number of Xbox pads** (1-4). Each pad is its own output (**Xbox 360 N**); only pads that are on are sent to. Lowering the number asks first, lists the bindings on the pads being turned off and keeps them (with a red warning in Map to Xbox). Map to Xbox lists the pads that are on
- **The Xbox Output View** now looks like Device Bindings: the Find bar and **Appearance…** on top, then one foldable section per pad that is on and bound, each with its live pad face, the devices driving it and its control list; live bars and dots while the profile runs; **Show only bound controls** (on at first, remembered). A red line with **Open Xbox Setup** shows only when ViGEmBus is missing. It replaces the Xbox Viewer window
- **Device Image**: give an input module's device its own picture with **Add Image…** (**Change Image…** once it has one) and **Remove Image**, on its Home card or in Module Setup. It shows on the card, in Module Setup and in the Device Library ahead of the background picture, and travels with Export / Import and Restore
- **Reset Picture** in the Device Mapping Editor (right-click Picture section, Arrange menu, Properties): puts a picture back to the size it had when added, centered where it is, with no crop, rotation, flip or skew and full opacity; snap points, layer, name, hidden and locked stay. One Undo step
- **Input Tester on the toolbar**, right after the vJoy Viewer
- **Home**
  - Compact view shows a small picture (or the device icon) on each card
  - Drag a selection box on empty space to pick cards (Shift adds), then **Hide Selected Cards** or **Reset Size of Selected Cards** (one Undo step each)
  - Undo and Redo are on both right-click menus (keys unchanged)
  - The OSC card's second line says what OSC is doing: "Listening on <address>:<port>", the last message received for 3 seconds, "Server not started: <reason>" or "Not listening"
- **OSC** shows its full name, **Open Sound Control (OSC)**, under the OSC page's title and as the Home card's tooltip
- **The OSC Monitor remembers** whether it was open, folded or closed, across leaving the OSC page and restarts. A Monitor left popped out comes back docked and open

### Changed
- **New words**: "Output not found" is now **Binding output not found** (Find tick, Home card "⚠ n binding outputs not found", the Open Profile notice); the Find hints say **custom name**; the Input Monitor says **no bindings**; the Find tick reads **No bindings in this mode**
- **One Input Tester at a time**: an Input Tester command while the tester is open updates what it checks and brings its window to the front instead of starting another. **Restart Input Tester** still opens a fresh one
- **Device arrows**: the ‹ › arrows in the page title also show on the Logical Device page and keep stepping through sticks, Keyboard, OSC and the Logical Device, with no dead end
- **vJoy Viewer** is a quick "did it register" check: OSC and the Logical Device get their cards, output bars follow bindings added while it is open, "No axes / buttons send to this output." shows when nothing does, and long chip names are shortened with "…"
- **Device Library**: built-in devices are always listed, rows have larger pictures and read "N saved setups", the divider is remembered, and an empty Library says so
- **Home**: Home cards can be made larger (up to 1080 x 780); reordering cards by drag looks like Device Bindings; the "N buttons N axes N hats" line is no longer shown on the Keyboard, OSC and Logical Device cards
- The Output Views no longer show the "View only" note under the title
- **OSC Setup › Server**: the list of this PC's addresses refreshes when the window comes to the front and every 5 seconds
- The old device tab row is gone (it could no longer be reached); Scripts and Profile Settings open from the View menu
- **Calibration**: the Raw field is read-only
- Autosaves are named "Autosave <time>.gmap"
- Help: HidHide hides HID devices only, not Xbox (XInput) controllers

### Fixed
- Saving a module in a folder with a very long path works
- Renaming a module keeps its card layout
- A saved macro delay of 0.05 s stays 0.05 s
- The Background Opacity slider in the Device Mapping Editor no longer logs warnings while dragged, and the chip tooltip sits just above the pointer at every zoom
- A rare crash at File › Exit (from the speech engine) is fixed
- The vJoy Viewer no longer crashes on a vJoy device without an id
- Reliability work: many fixes to the automated tests and their tools, so problems are caught sooner
