## What's new in 1.1.1

### New
- **Drivers in setup**: setup's new **Drivers** page lists vJoy, ViGEmBus and HidHide with their installed versions, installs missing drivers and offers updates. It also sets up your **vJoy devices** (axes, buttons and hats) and installs the **Microsoft Visual C++ Runtime** first when it's missing
- **Tools › Drivers…**: check, install and update the drivers and change your **vJoy devices** at any time, without running setup again
- **vJoy devices** are chosen row by row: tick each axis by name, pick the buttons and hats, add a device with the **+ Add device** row at the end of the list, remove one with its ✕. A new device counts down in buttons (vJoy 1 = 128, vJoy 2 = 127, ...) so Gremlin-Platforms can tell them apart, and you're warned when two devices would look the same
- A vJoy device another program is using shows its name in red; changing or removing it asks first
- **Start with Windows**: in Options › Startup and Tray and in the tray icon's menu. Gremlin-Platforms opens minimized when you sign in to Windows; if Windows' own Startup Apps page has it turned off, Gremlin-Platforms shows that and opens Windows' page
- **Updating vJoy keeps your vJoy devices**: Gremlin-Platforms checks them after the update and offers to repair any that were lost
- Fix a binding from Search: **Remove binding…** and **Change target…** work even when the device isn't plugged in; **Undo** brings it back
- **Health**: a new toolbar button (after Search) turns red with the number of bindings whose output isn't found in the open profile. Its window lists the problems (**Binding output not found**, **Used twice**, **No bindings**, **Not plugged in**), says why in the **Why** panel, and fixes them from the right-click menu, several at once if you pick them with Ctrl+click or Shift+click; **Undo** takes the whole change back

### Changed
- **Help** is rewritten: every topic says what it's for, then gives numbered steps, with shorter sentences and the same words as the screen. The Help window is easier to read: a narrower text column, clear subheadings, spacing between list items and brighter links
- Axis names are the same everywhere: X Axis, Y Axis, Z Axis, X Rotation, Y Rotation, Z Rotation, **Slider 1** and **Slider 2** (short: X, Y, Z, Rx, Ry, Rz, S1, S2)
- **Screen Saver Mode**: **Test now** is no longer cancelled by moving the mouse; a key press, mouse click or wheel still cancels it
- **Screen Saver Mode** runs one screen saver at a time
- While a profile runs, Tools › Drivers asks to stop it before it changes the vJoy devices
- HidHide updates run in two steps with a restart in between, and say so before they start
- **Search** has a cleaner layout: **Listen** is the first button, your recent searches open in the drop-down under the search box (with **Clear recent searches**), the columns line up, long actions are cut in the middle so the number stays, and the problem filters and marks moved to **Health**

### Fixed
- The Start-menu shortcut could disappear after an update
- The driver signature check failed on vJoy's signer name ("Zürich")
- A vJoy device with no axes ticked could be set up with every axis; at least one axis now stays ticked
- The Open Profile message names an unplugged device instead of showing its ID
- **Turn displays off now** in the tray menu was cancelled by the click and the mouse moving after it; mouse movement is now ignored until the displays are off and for 2 seconds after
