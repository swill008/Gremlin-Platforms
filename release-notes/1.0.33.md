## What's new in 1.0.33

### New
- **One folder per device**: everything about a device lives in its own folder, named after the name you gave it
  - Its settings, its device map (both planes and every layer), its pictures and its recovery copy are kept together and saved in place
  - Renaming a device renames its folder; if a file in it is open elsewhere, the rename is refused with the reason
  - A picture whose file is gone shows a grey **Picture missing** placeholder with **Choose Picture…** on its right-click menu
  - Edits in the **Device Mapping Editor** wait aside until **Save**; **Cancel** leaves the device untouched, and Save removes pictures nothing uses
  - Templates export as one .gtemplate file
- **Your data moves itself once**: at the first start, your settings, devices and Device Library are moved to the new layout automatically, after a backup copy is made. Nothing is lost and there is nothing to do
- **Background and foreground in the device map**
  - The **background** is the artwork behind everything (pictures, shapes and text, in as many layers as you like); the **foreground** holds the chips, leaders, hotspots and the rest. The device's photo is now the bottom background picture
  - **Edit › Background**: **Edit Background** (Ctrl+B), **Add Picture to Background…**, **Clear Background**, **Fit to Photo Frame**; **Edit Background** is also on the empty-space right-click menu
  - In Background mode only background items can be picked; the foreground shows faintly (**Show foreground faintly** in the banner); **Done** or Esc leaves it
  - **Move to Background** / **Move to Foreground** (Ctrl+Shift+B) with Undo; chips snapped to a background picture follow it
  - Every picture gets a **Look** section (Brightness, Contrast, Greyscale, Opacity) and **Reset Shape**
  - **Layers** ends with a folded **Background** row (eye, lock, Opacity)
  - **Print & Export**: **Include background** tick; the old "Background" colour setting is now **Page**
  - **Copy Device Map** has "Copy the background too"; Export, the Device Library, History and the Home cards carry every background layer
- **The Device Mapping Editor's menu bar holds every command**: File, Edit, View, Insert, Arrange, Leader, Format and Help, each command with its usual key (Cut, Select All, Zoom In and Zoom Out added)
  - The tool rows are gone; **Device Map Options** (Ctrl+,) and **Keyboard Shortcuts** (Ctrl+K) open as small windows with a pin and an ×; unpinned, they close on a click elsewhere
  - **Keyboard Shortcuts** lists every key grouped by menu, then the mouse
  - **Chips** sits on the left and **Layers** (with **Properties** under it) on the right as side panels that fold to a slim edge; hover to peek, click to open, pin to keep beside the map, drag the inner edge to resize. **View › Reset Panels** puts them back
- **More on the right-click menu**: **Cut**, **Paste Here** (at the spot you clicked), **Select All**, **Show in Layers**, **Properties…** (F4) and **Repeat** of your last command
- **Favourites**: click the ☆ on any command (right-click menu or menu bar) to list it at the top of the right-click menu; × removes it. **Device Map Options › Right-click menu** sets how many show (0–15, 5 by default)
- **The device's picture is the whole background** (input modules): the Home cards, Module Setup, the HidHide window, the Device Library and the Export / Import previews show every visible background layer together, as in the Device Mapping Editor, instead of only the bottom picture. It is made again by itself whenever the background changes
- **Print & Export at 100 %** uses the background picture with the most detail, so a detailed picture on a higher layer exports at full sharpness
- **Handles and keys work like Photoshop** in the Device Mapping Editor
  - A corner keeps a picture's or shape's proportions; hold **Shift** to resize freely. Side handles stretch one way
  - **Alt** on a handle resizes from the center; **Alt** while drawing draws a shape from the center
  - **Alt**+drag an item to copy it; **Shift** while moving keeps the move on a straight line; **Ctrl** while dragging skips snapping
  - **Ctrl+Shift**+drag a side handle to skew; dragging a handle past the opposite edge flips the item
  - Crop: **Shift** keeps the crop's proportions, **Enter** finishes, **Esc** puts it back
  - **Esc** during any drag puts things back as they were, with no Undo step
  - While dragging, a small box shows the size, angle or distance; the pointer shows which way a handle resizes
  - Several selected items get resize handles of their own
  - **Properties** has a link between Width and Height (on by default) that keeps the proportions
  - **Reset Shape** also gives a picture back its own proportions
  - **Shift**+arrow nudges 10 px; lines and path segments with **Shift** keep to 45° steps
- **Home Undo / Redo** for card size, order and stacks, card pictures, **Reset Card Layout**, **Compact view**, **Layout** and the divider (up to 50 steps; switching profile clears them)
- **Help** has a **Background and foreground** topic, and its **Show me** links can now point inside the Device Mapping Editor

### Changed
- **New names**, on screen, in Help and in messages
  - The Configuration page is **Device Bindings**
  - The Auto Mapper is the **Auto Mapping Tool**
  - The Button Map is the **Device Mapping Editor** ("Mapping Editor" on the toolbar); what you make in it is a **device map**, and its settings are **Device Map Options**
- **One Export / Import Device window**: **Tools › Device Setup › Export / Import Device**, or **Export Device…** / **Import Device…** on a Home card
  - One file type, **.gmap**; the old Device Pack .zip is no longer written or read, and the Device Library saves and imports .gmap only
  - A .gmap carries **Made by**, a **Note**, the modes you choose (and only the actions they reach) and the output modules' pictures
  - Import **As a new device** (a twin with a taken name gets "(2)" and keeps its own bindings) or **Onto an existing device** (with a preview and warning); the default follows whether the device is already here
  - **Restore Import** takes back either kind of import, after asking
- **The History window stays still**
  - Restoring marks the entry in place ("Restored · 19:47", "Reapplied" if undone) instead of adding a row; changes made meanwhile wait behind "N new changes · Show"
  - Entries sit under day headings, and repeated changes to the same thing fold into one row
  - The detail says in plain words what restoring does, with **Restore to before this change** and **Restore to after this change**; the full before and after is one click away
- **View only**: while you aren't editing, a slim line under the menu bar says so, with an **Edit Mapping** link; with no device chosen, it tells you to choose one from **File › Device** first
- **Moving needs a small drag**: a click on a chip, picture, drawing or table cell only selects it; nothing moves (and no Undo step is made) until the mouse travels a few pixels
- **One Undo step per drag**: moving, resizing, rotating, cropping, a leader end or a guide is always one step
- The Undo / Redo row and the Foreground | Background switch under the menus are gone; Undo and Redo stay in the Edit menu, the right-click menu and Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y
- A closed hand shows while you pan the map; middle-drag and Space+drag pan while editing
- Insert's picture item reads **Import Picture…**
- Home: **Compact view**, **Layout** and its list sit on the left after the divider
- New toolbar icons for the vJoy Viewer, the Device Mapping Editor and the Device Library

### Fixed
- A picture's handles stay on the picture while you resize, crop, rotate or skew it; a picture added with **Import Picture…** keeps its own proportions, and **Choose Picture…** fits the box to the new picture. A picture added earlier that looks stretched is put right by **Reset Shape**
- In the Device Mapping Editor you can click, right-click, select and drag anywhere in the view at any zoom, also beside and below the page; the grid now reaches past the page with a border at its edge
- Chip names and text on the map stay sharp when zoomed in, even in a small window
- The History window folds repeated changes to the same device file into one row again
- A chip dragged from the **Chips** panel drops centered on the pointer, with or without **Chip only**
- The Xbox Viewer shows the pad picture again, and says "Xbox face image missing" if it can't load
- The picture folder you last used is remembered again
- Restoring an older Device Library History entry puts back only that change; later changes stay
- An edit whose changes were all undone no longer leaves a recovery copy behind
- Save always finishes cleanly, even when a picture is still in use
- A leader end dropped on a picture's snap point stays there
- Paste goes into the plane you are editing
- Shift- or Ctrl+right-click on an item adds it to the selection, so the menu acts on what it names
- Backspace while drawing a path removes the last point, not the selection; Esc stops drawing wherever the keyboard is
- A table's cell target is checked after Undo or a change of table
- Keys pressed in Device Map Options or Keyboard Shortcuts no longer run editor commands
- A side panel keeps your pin after a narrow window folds it
- Greyed **Move to Background** / **Move to Foreground** in the menu bar says why
- An unplugged twin no longer writes into the other twin's folder
- Two pastes get two names; **Rename Template** no longer half-renames
- The Input Tester keeps writing its log when Windows refuses to roll it over
